# ⚔️ Weapon X Simulator is officially OUT!

It's finally here. After months of development, **Weapon X Simulator** is live on Roblox and ready for you to jump in.

Collect mythic swords, fight your way through zones, and become the most powerful warrior on the server. Here's everything that's waiting for you at launch.

---

## 🏆 The Hub, Merging & Upgrading

![Hub area with leaderboards and portals](images/devlogs/weaponx-release/hub.png)

The hub is where it all starts. Access the global leaderboards, head through portals into the world, and see the top players' all-time gold and gem highs. Think you have what it takes to crack the top 10?

Picked up duplicates along the way? Don't vendor them — **merge** them. Combine two of the same weapon to forge a stronger version. Once you've got the one you want, push it even further through the **upgrade system**. The ceiling is high, so keep climbing.

---

## 🌍 45 Stages & Zones at Launch

Fight through **45 stages and zones** at launch, each with its own enemies, atmosphere, and challenges. We're just getting started — more zones are coming in future updates so the grind never stops.

![Scarecrow enemies in a zone](images/devlogs/weaponx-release/zone-enemies.png)

---

## 👹 Area Bosses

![Forest guardian area boss](images/devlogs/weaponx-release/boss.png)

Each area has its own **boss** — a towering enemy with serious HP and a unique move set. Defeat them for a chance at **special loot drops and exclusive boss chest weapons** you can't find anywhere else. Some of the rarest weapons in the game only come from boss chests, so learning the fights is worth your time.

---

## ⏳ Limited Time Weapons — Once They're Gone, They're Gone

![Dragonslayer limited weapon — Stock: 9999/10000](images/devlogs/weaponx-release/limited-weapon.png)

**Limited time weapons** are in the game right now with finite stock. Once the stock hits zero, that weapon is gone **permanently** — it will never come back. The **Dragonslayer** is one of the first limited weapons available at launch with only 10,000 in existence. If you want it, get it now before it's history.
